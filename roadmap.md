# 🌐 Stage‑1 Roadmap

U.S. Domestic Remedy Exhaustion — Human‑Readable Overview

This roadmap outlines the full architecture of Stage 1: U.S. Domestic Remedy Exhaustion, including all layers, deliverables, Python toolkits, and jurisdictional context. It is the human‑readable counterpart to roadmap.json.

---

🇺🇸 Jurisdictional Reality (Core Context)

This project exists because:

- U.S. nationals are not subject to ICC jurisdiction  
- The United States is not subject to Inter‑American Court jurisdiction  
- UN treaty bodies (UNCAT, ICCPR) require domestic exhaustion  
- Transnational repression requires structured documentation  

Therefore, all domestic mechanisms must be mapped, documented, and exhausted before any international review is possible.
